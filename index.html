Research Community Personal

Hi :) I’m Veronica Xia!

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at McGill University.

I am supervised by Dr. Jin Guo, and broadly speaking, I work in human-computer interaction to understand how algorithmic systems are influenced by and exacerbate existing social forces. I am also interested in AI governance and responsible AI.

I am supposed by FRQNT, and I am affiliated with Mila (Quebec AI Institute). Previously, I have interned at Hitachi Energy Research, Microsoft and Huawei, and I completed my B.Sc. at McGill University in Honours Math and Computer Science.
